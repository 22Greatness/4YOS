# Goal
Write and simplify ratios, share an amount in a ratio, compare prices with unit rates, and solve direct and inverse proportion problems.

# Why
Every recipe, map scale, exchange rate and "best buy" decision is a ratio. Proportion lets you work out an unknown amount from a known one, and it's one of the most useful tools you'll get. Statistics compares groups with ratios and rates, and in computing you'll meet them as speed, bandwidth and how a program's time grows with its input.

?? Simplify $\frac{12}{18}$.
=> $\frac{2}{3}$.

?? What is $\frac{3}{5}$ of ₦20,000?
=> ₦12,000.

# Lesson
## What a ratio is
A **ratio** compares amounts. If a class has 12 women and 8 men, the ratio of women to men is 12 to 8, written $12 : 8$.

Order matters: women to men is $12 : 8$, and men to women is $8 : 12$.

### Simplifying
A ratio works like a fraction: divide all parts by the same number. Both 12 and 8 divide by 4, so $12 : 8 = 3 : 2$. It means that for every 3 women there are 2 men.

The units must match before you simplify. To compare 50 kobo with ₦2, first write them both in kobo: $50 : 200 = 1 : 4$.

### Parts and fractions of the whole
The ratio $3 : 2$ has $3 + 2 = 5$ parts in all. So women are $\frac{3}{5}$ of the class, and men are $\frac{2}{5}$.

> Mistake: Reading $3 : 2$ as "$\frac{3}{2}$ of the class are women". The ratio compares part with part. For a fraction of the whole, divide by the total of the parts.

?? A bag has 6 red and 9 blue pens. What's the ratio of red to blue, in simplest form? What fraction are red?
=> $6 : 9 = 2 : 3$. Red is $\frac{2}{5}$.

## Sharing in a ratio
To share ₦60,000 in the ratio $3 : 2$:

1. Add the parts: $3 + 2 = 5$.
2. Find one part: $60{,}000 \div 5 = 12{,}000$.
3. Multiply: $3 \times 12{,}000 = 36{,}000$, and $2 \times 12{,}000 = 24{,}000$. So the shares are ₦36,000 and ₦24,000.
4. Check that they add back: $36{,}000 + 24{,}000 = 60{,}000$.

It works the same with three parts. Sharing ₦1,200 in the ratio $1 : 2 : 3$ means 6 parts, ₦200 each, which gives ₦200, ₦400 and ₦600.

## Rates and best buys
A **rate** compares two different kinds of quantity: naira per litre, kilometres per hour, megabytes per second. A **unit rate** has 1 as the second quantity.

To compare two prices, get them onto the same unit.

| Bundle | Price | Size | Naira per GB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | ₦1,500 | 3 GB | $1{,}500 \div 3 = 500$ |
| B | ₦3,500 | 8 GB | $3{,}500 \div 8 = 437.50$ |

These prices are invented, to show the method. B costs more in total, but it's cheaper per GB. Whether B is better depends on whether you'll use it all before it expires.

### Speed
Speed is a rate: distance ÷ time. A bus that goes 150 km in 2.5 hours travels at $150 \div 2.5 = 60$ km per hour. Exchange rates work the same way, and so does a download.

To download 900 MB at 3 MB per second takes $900 \div 3 = 300$ seconds, which is 5 minutes.

> Mistake: Mixing bits and bytes. Internet speeds are usually in megabits per second, and file sizes in megabytes. One byte is 8 bits, so a 40 Mb per second connection gives about 5 MB per second.

## Direct proportion
Two quantities are in **direct proportion** when one is always the same multiple of the other. Doubling one then doubles the other, and their ratio stays the same. If 4 books cost ₦6,000, how much do 7 cost?

The clearest way is through one unit. One book costs $6{,}000 \div 4 = 1{,}500$, so ₦1,500. So 7 books cost $7 \times 1{,}500 = 10{,}500$, which is ₦10,500.

You can also write equal fractions and fill in the gap:

$$\frac{6{,}000}{4} = \frac{?}{7}$$

Multiplying across gives the same result. Later on, you'll see that direct proportion draws a straight line through zero.

### Scales
A map with scale $1 : 50{,}000$ means 1 cm on the map is 50,000 cm on the ground, which is 500 m. So 4 cm on the map is 2 km.

## Inverse proportion
Sometimes, one thing goes up as the other goes down. If 4 people take 6 days to do a job, how long will 8 people take? Not 12 days! More people means **less** time. This is **inverse proportion**, where doubling one halves the other, and it's the product that stays fixed.

The total work is $4 \times 6 = 24$ person-days. So 8 people take $24 \div 8 = 3$ days, and 3 people take $24 \div 3 = 8$ days.

| People | Days | People × days |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 12 | 24 |
| 4 | 6 | 24 |
| 8 | 3 | 24 |

Before calculating, ask: if this goes up, does that go up too, or down?

> Mistake: Using direct proportion by reflex. Think about what really happens. For a fixed distance, speed and travel time are inversely related: twice as fast takes half the time.

### Limits of a model
Proportion is a model. It assumes things stay steady. One woman can have a baby in nine months, and nine women can't have one in a month. Eight people can't always work four times as fast as two, since they get in each other's way. Use your judgement about whether the rule really applies.

# Exercises
?? Simplify $45 : 60$.
=> The HCF is 15, so $3 : 4$.

?? Share ₦48,000 in the ratio $5 : 3$.
=> 8 parts, ₦6,000 each: ₦30,000 and ₦18,000.

?? 5 kg of rice costs ₦7,500, and 2 kg costs ₦3,200. Which is cheaper per kg?
=> $7{,}500 \div 5 = 1{,}500$, so ₦1,500 per kg. $3{,}200 \div 2 = 1{,}600$, so ₦1,600 per kg. The 5 kg bag is cheaper. These prices are invented.

?? If 3 metres of cloth cost ₦4,200, how much do 8 metres cost?
=> One metre costs ₦1,400. So 8 metres cost ₦11,200.

?? A car takes 4 hours at 60 km per hour. How long does the same trip take at 80 km per hour?
=> The distance is $4 \times 60 = 240$ km. $240 \div 80 = 3$ hours. It's inverse proportion.

# Practice
1. Compare two real bundles, bags or packs of something you buy, using naira per unit. Which is really cheaper? Is the cheaper one sensible, given how you'd use it?
2. Think of a real amount shared between things you pay for. Write the ratio, simplify it, and write each as a fraction of the whole.
3. Time a real task, such as reading one page. Use direct proportion to estimate how long a chapter would take. Then test the estimate by really doing it, and write down how close you got.
4. Name one real situation that's inversely proportional, and one that looks proportional but really isn't.

Write your unit prices, your estimate against reality, and your two examples.

# Rubric
- I compared real prices by the unit, and said which made sense.
- I tested a real proportion estimate against what really happened.
- I can tell direct from inverse proportion, and where the model fails.

# Summary
- A ratio compares part with part. Divide all parts by the same number to simplify, and match units first.
- To share in a ratio, add the parts, find one part, then multiply.
- A unit rate lets you compare different sizes fairly.
- Direct proportion: double one, double the other, as the ratio stays fixed.
- Inverse proportion: double one, halve the other, as the product stays fixed.
- Ask whether the model really applies.

Q: A team has 10 women and 15 men. What fraction are women, in the form a/b?
P: What a ratio is
A: 2/5
= $10 : 15 = 2 : 3$, which is 5 parts. Women are 2 of them.

Q: Share ₦{k}0,000 in the ratio $2 : 3$. What's the larger share? Enter the number only, without the ₦ sign.
P: Sharing in a ratio
Vars: k=1..9
F: k*6000
= 5 parts, so one part is ₦{k*2000}. The larger share is 3 parts: ₦{k*6000}.

Q: Share 800 in the ratio $1 : 3 : 4$. What's the smallest share?
P: Sharing in a ratio
A: 100
= 8 parts, so one part is 100.

Q: Which is cheaper per unit? Pack A is 6 for ₦900. Pack B is 4 for ₦640.
P: Rates and best buys
- Pack B, at ₦160 each
* Pack A, at ₦150 each
- They cost the same per unit
= $900 \div 6 = 150$, and $640 \div 4 = 160$.

Q: 5 notebooks cost ₦3,000. How much do 8 cost, in naira?
P: Direct proportion
A: 4800 | 4,800 | ₦4,800 | ₦4800
= One costs ₦600, so 8 cost ₦4,800.

Q: 3 people take 8 days to paint a building. At the same rate, how many days do 6 people take?
P: Inverse proportion
A: 4 | 4 days | four
= The work is 24 person-days, so $24 \div 6 = 4$.

Q: Which situation shows inverse proportion?
P: Inverse proportion
- The number of books you buy, and the total you pay
* The speed of a car, and the time for a fixed journey
- The number of hours you work, and your pay per hour worked
= As speed rises, the time falls, with their product staying fixed.

Q[transfer]: Your connection is 40 megabits per second, and the download keeps that rate. How long does a 100 megabyte file take, in seconds?
P: Rates and best buys
A: 20 | 20 seconds
= 40 Mb per second is 5 MB per second. $100 \div 5 = 20$.

Q[transfer]: A map has a scale of $1 : 25{,}000$. Two towns are 8 cm apart on it. How far apart are they, in km?
P: Direct proportion
A: 2 | 2 km
= $8 \times 25{,}000 = 200{,}000$ cm, which is 2,000 m, or 2 km.

Q[transfer]: A program takes 2 seconds to handle 1,000 records, and someone predicts 2,000 seconds for 1,000,000. Which is the best response?
P: Inverse proportion
- It's exact, because every program's time is directly proportional to its input
* It's a useful first guess, but only if the time really grows in step with the input
- It's wrong, because time always falls as the size of the input gets larger
= Proportion is a model. Some programs get far slower than that as the input grows, which you'll study in Data Structures and Algorithms.

# Cards
What does a ratio compare? :: Part with part, in a fixed order. $3 : 2$ means 3 for every 2.
What's the fraction of the whole in the ratio $3 : 2$? :: $\frac{3}{5}$ and $\frac{2}{5}$, since there are 5 parts.
How do you share in a ratio? :: Add the parts, find one part, then multiply.
What is a unit rate? :: The amount for one unit, such as naira per GB.

What stays fixed in inverse proportion? :: The product. Double one, and you halve the other.
