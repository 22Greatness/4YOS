## NUC CCMAS 2023
- CCMAS fixes about 70% of each programme, and the university adds about 30%.
- It applies to 100-level entrants from 2023/24.
- Full text: `official/ccmas_computing.txt` and `official/ccmas_sciences.txt`.
- Shared 100-level core: GST 111, GST 112, MTH 101, MTH 102, PHY 101, PHY 102, PHY 107, PHY 108, STA 111, COS 101, COS 102.
- General courses: GST 111, 112, 212, 312 and ENT 211, 312. These are now graded.

## Grading
**5-point scale:** A 70+ = 5, B 60–69 = 4, C 50–59 = 3, D 45–49 = 2, E 40–44 = 1, F below 40 = 0.

**Degree classes:** First 4.50–5.00, 2:1 3.50–4.49, 2:2 2.40–3.49, Third 1.50–2.39, Pass 1.00–1.49.

**Assessment and carry-overs:**
- Continuous assessment counts 30–40% and the exam 60–70%.
- A failed course is a carry-over. It must be retaken, and the F stays in the CGPA.

**Result slip:**
- Each course row: code, title, units, score, grade, grade point, and units × points.
- Totals: TCU, TGP and GPA, then the same three cumulatively, ending in CGPA.
- At the bottom: outstanding courses and a remark.

## Legal (not legal advice)
- **Facts can be used.** Course codes, titles and units are data under the Copyright Act 2022 s.3. Handbook and CCMAS wording is protected.
- **Naming the university:** factual naming is fine. No logo, no "official" wording, and add a not-affiliated notice. The risk otherwise is passing off: Trade Marks Act s.3; Patkun v Niger Shoes, 1998.
- **GPL tools:** CircuitJS (GPL-2), webR (GPL-3) and the Linux image's packages ship as separate programs, with their source.
