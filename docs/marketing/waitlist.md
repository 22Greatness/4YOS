## 1. Waitlist Announcement & Copy

### Header
**Join the 4YOS Pilot Cohort (First 50 Students)**

### Body Copy
We are opening 4YOS to an initial group of 20 to 50 dedicated students from Nigerian universities and polytechnics. As a pilot participant, you get:
1. **Free Early Access:** Full access to all 100-Level Computer Science, Mathematics, and Statistics courses.
2. **Direct Line to the Creators:** Report confusing explanations and request specific past topics; your feedback directly shapes the lesson drafts.
3. **Founder's Discount Code:** Permanent 20% discount on all future degree level packs when 4YOS launches campus-wide.

## 2. Intake Form Fields

| Field Name | Type | Options / Format | Mandatory | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fullName` | Text | First and Last Name | Yes | Account personalization and certificate of completion. |
| `phone` | Phone / Tel | E.164 Nigerian format (`+234...` or `080...`) | Yes | SMS OTP challenge delivery and WhatsApp pilot group invite. |
| `institution` | Select + Other | UNIPORT, UNILAG, ABU, FUTO, NOUN, Federal Poly Nekede, Other | Yes | Mapping your exact departmental curriculum and grading rules. |
| `programme` | Select + Other | Computer Science, Software Engineering, Mathematics, Cyber Security, Other | Yes | Programme map assignment. |
| `currentLevel` | Radio | 100L, 200L (Direct Entry), 200L+, Self-Learner | Yes | Identifying foundation course needs. |
| `primaryDevice` | Select | Budget Android Phone, Mid/High Android, iPhone, Windows Laptop, Mac | Yes | Ensuring test coverage across physical hardware. |
| `biggestStruggle` | Text (Short) | e.g. "Understanding Python loops", "Passing MTH 101" | No | Prioritizing lesson write-ups. |
