## 2. Problem & Value Proposition

### The Problem Students Face
1. **Unreliable Campus Internet:** Lecture slides and materials are scattered across WhatsApp groups and slow portals. When data runs out before exams, study stops.
2. **Abstract Lectures:** First-year lecturers often assume you already know programming or advanced algebra. Concepts are skipped without foundations.
3. **GPA Mystery:** Students don't know where they stand until results drop. Hand-calculated GPAs often misinterpret grade boundaries.

### The 4YOS Solution
- **The Winding Path:** One clear Next Action at every step. No choice paralysis.
- **Teaches From First Basics:** Every topic starts with hand-worked examples, diagrams, and step-by-step reasoning before code or formulas.
- **Immediate Feedback:** Practice checks check your real working instantly and show why wrong options fail.
- **Real Grade Projection:** Honest practice estimate that calculates your exact GPA, credit points, and cleared fails under your institution's real regulations.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Does 4YOS need data to run?**
A: Only once to install and download your course packs (each pack is under 15 MB). After that, 4YOS operates 100% offline for up to 60 days before a lightweight security check-in.

**Q: Can I use it on my itel or Tecno phone?**
A: Yes. 4YOS is heavily optimized for budget Android devices (Android 8+), requiring less than 2 MB of initial download and under 50 MB of device storage.

**Q: Are these official university exams?**
A: No. Every assessment result clearly displays: *"Practice estimate, not an official result."* 4YOS prepares you for your institution's real exams using verified curriculum standards.

**Q: How many devices can I use with my account?**
A: Each account supports up to 2 active devices simultaneously (e.g. your phone and a laptop). You can deactivate an old device in Settings if you switch phones.

**Q: Is there an AI tutor that makes things up?**
A: No. In version 1, 4YOS has no generative hallucinating chatbot. All lessons and feedback are authored by subject specialists and follow rigorous curriculum checks.
